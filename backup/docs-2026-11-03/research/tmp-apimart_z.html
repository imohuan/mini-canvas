Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Z Image Turbo API

What is Z Image Turbo and who developed it?

Z Image Turbo is an ultra-fast image generation model developed by Alibaba. It excels at generating high-quality images from text prompts with significantly faster processing times compared to many competing models.

What image formats and aspect ratios does Z Image Turbo support?

Z Image Turbo supports seven aspect ratios: 1:1 (square), 16:9 (widescreen), 9:16 (vertical), 4:3 (standard), 3:4 (portrait standard), 3:2 (landscape), and 2:3 (portrait). Each ratio is available in both 1K and 2K resolution.

How do I write effective prompts for Z Image Turbo?

Be specific about visual details including colors, lighting, composition, and artistic style. Keep prompts within 800 characters and enable prompt_extend when you want to expand the description. The model handles complex, multi-object scenes well.

Can I use Z Image Turbo to edit existing images?

This endpoint currently supports text-to-image generation only. It does not accept reference images or PNG masks. Describe the image you want in a text prompt.

What is APIMart's pricing for Z Image Turbo API?

Each request generates one image. Standard generation and prompt_extend use their respective current per-image prices and discounts shown in this page’s pricing details. The prompt_extend price covers the complete image generation.

Is the Z Image Turbo API free to try?

Yes, you get free generations daily to test stability and image quality before committing to paid usage.

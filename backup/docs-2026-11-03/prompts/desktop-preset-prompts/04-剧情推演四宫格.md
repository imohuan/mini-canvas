# 剧情推演四宫格

**LibTV 公开说明**：生成四格剧情推演，直接基于当前图像做 4 宫格剧情推演；支持文生图 / 参考图。

**什么时候用**：已经有一张画面，想知道“接下来会发生什么”。适合短剧、漫画、产品故事。

## 可直接粘贴的模板

```text
Create a 2x2 story continuation grid with four clearly separated panels, based on the
supplied reference image. The four panels show one continuous chain of cause and effect.

IDENTITY LOCK: preserve the exact character face, hairstyle, skin tone, body proportions,
outfit, and props from the reference image. Do not redesign or restyle the character.
SCENE LOCK: preserve the location, architecture, palette, time of day, and lighting direction.

PANEL 1 (top-left) — the supplied moment: [describe the current pose and framing].
PANEL 2 (top-right) — immediate reaction: [small physical change, one new element].
PANEL 3 (bottom-left) — complication: [the action turns; stronger camera angle].
PANEL 4 (bottom-right) — consequence / payoff: [new state, clean readable silhouette].

Logic rules: every change must follow physically from the previous panel. No teleporting,
no sudden costume change, no new main character. Camera can change angle and distance,
but screen direction stays consistent. Thin white gutters, no text, no captions, no watermark.
Cinematic storytelling stills, same grade in all four panels. Aspect ratio 1:1.
```

## 填写要点

- 第 2 格负责“反应”，第 3 格负责“转折”，第 4 格负责“结果”；这是四格最稳的节奏。
- 不确定剧情时，只写 `PANEL 2-4: infer the most physically plausible continuation`，让模型自己推。
- 要做搞笑漫，把 `cinematic storytelling stills` 改成 `deadpan comic panels, clean line art`。

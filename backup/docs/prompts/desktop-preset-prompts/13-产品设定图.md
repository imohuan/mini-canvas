# 产品设定图

**LibTV 公开说明**：产品多角度与细节拆解；直接生成当前图像的产品细节特写。

**什么时候用**：一个商品要在多张海报、详情页、分镜里反复出现；先锁住形状、材质、标签。

## 可直接粘贴的模板

```text
Create a product reference sheet on a seamless #DCDCDC light-gray background.
One organized grid, shadowless studio lighting, no gradients, no reflections.

PRODUCT IDENTITY LOCK: [product name and category]. Preserve the exact silhouette,
proportions, material, color values, seams, hardware, label placement, and logo geometry
from the supplied image. Do not redesign, restyle, beautify, or add branding.

LAYOUT — [6] orthographic views plus [2] macro details:
front / back / left side / right side / top / bottom / three-quarter hero.
Consistent scale and vertical alignment across views, whole product visible, no cropping.
Macro 1: [material weave / texture close-up].
Macro 2: [label, seam, or construction detail].

CAMERA: 100mm macro lens at f/8, ISO 100, product fully sharp front to back, neutral white balance.
REALISM: visible micro texture, raised material structure, tight surface density, natural
micro-shadowing, material compression at seams, texture following the product's curvature.

No props, no environment, no hands, no text overlays beyond the product's own label,
no watermark. [4-6] tiny color swatches with hex codes in the lower margin.
Aspect ratio 1:1.
```

## 填写要点

- 产品设定图要“准”，不是“好看”：先写禁止 redesign / beautify，再谈光线。
- 六个正交视图比四个更稳；俯视和底视能防止包装被画成平板。
- 标签文字容易乱码；能后期贴字就要求 `blank label area, no rendered text`。

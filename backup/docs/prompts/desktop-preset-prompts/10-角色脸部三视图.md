# 角色脸部三视图

**LibTV 公开说明**：基于一张参考图生成脸部细节三视图，白底，带正面肖像特写。

**什么时候用**：脸部是重点，需要统一眉眼、鼻型、下颌和发际线。

## 可直接粘贴的模板

```text
Character face reference sheet of [character name], generated from the supplied image.
White background, three head-and-shoulders views arranged left to right:
front view, three-quarter view (turned 45 degrees), side profile (turned 90 degrees).

IDENTITY LOCK: preserve the exact face geometry, eye shape and color, eyebrows, nose,
lip shape, jawline, ears, skin tone, freckles/moles, and hairstyle from the reference.
Same age and same expression baseline (relaxed, mouth closed, eyes open) in all three views.

CAMERA LOCK: same focal length, same camera height at eye level, same head size in frame,
same soft even studio lighting from both sides, same white balance. No perspective distortion.

Focus on facial structure rather than styling: neutral makeup, no jewelry that hides the face,
no hats, no dramatic shadows. Hair may reveal the ears and hairline in the profile view.
No text, no labels, no watermark, no extra people. Photorealistic skin texture with visible
pores; do not over-smooth or beautify. Aspect ratio 3:4.
```

## 参考图很糊时

先生成一张清晰的正面肖像，再把这张当参考图；不要直接在模糊图上做三视图，会把噪点当成痣。

## 填写要点

- 四分之三角度写 `45 degrees`，侧脸写 `90 degrees`；角度模糊最容易变成三张不同的人。
- 三张脸要同一表情基线；如果一张笑、一张严肃，模型会改脸型。
- 发型不对称时，明确写“profile view exposes the left/right ear”。

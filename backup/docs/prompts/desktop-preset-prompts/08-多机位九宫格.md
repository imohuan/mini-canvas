# 多机位九宫格

**LibTV 公开说明**：生成多视角机位图，基于当前图生成约 9 个不同角度、景别的视角。

**什么时候用**：同一场景需要正反打、俯仰、远近，先一次看全。

## 可直接粘贴的模板

```text
Create a 3x3 multi-camera contact sheet with nine clearly separated panels of the SAME
moment in the SAME scene, each from a different camera setup.

SOURCE LOCK: use the supplied reference image for the exact character identity, wardrobe,
props, architecture, materials, palette, and light direction. Every panel must depict the
same instant; only the camera changes. Do not add or remove objects between panels.

PANEL 1 (top-left): extreme wide, high angle — full environment and scale.
PANEL 2 (top-center): wide, eye level — establishing composition.
PANEL 3 (top-right): medium wide, three-quarter left — spatial relationship.
PANEL 4 (middle-left): medium, three-quarter right — action readable.
PANEL 5 (center): medium close-up, eye level — emotional anchor.
PANEL 6 (middle-right): close-up, low angle — dramatic emphasis.
PANEL 7 (bottom-left): over-the-shoulder — point of view toward the subject.
PANEL 8 (bottom-center): profile, long lens — compressed background.
PANEL 9 (bottom-right): top-down or dutch angle — graphic final option.

Thin white gutters, no text, no labels, no arrows, no watermark. Consistent lighting
logic and color grade across all nine panels. Photorealistic cinematic stills.
Aspect ratio 1:1.
```

## 填写要点

- 九格是**同一时刻**的不同机位，不是九个连续动作；想讲故事请用「故事板」。
- 第 9 格用俯视或荷兰角做“备用构图”，方便后期挑图。
- 如果模型把人物画成九个不同的人，把 `SOURCE LOCK` 加粗并重复人物细节。

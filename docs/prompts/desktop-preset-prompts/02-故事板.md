# 故事板（6 格连续分镜）

**LibTV 公开说明**：描述一个 10–15 秒内能演完的小剧情片段，系统整理为 4–8 个连续分镜；内容过长会自动提炼关键剧情。

**什么时候用**：已经知道剧情，要一次看到完整镜头顺序。

## 可直接粘贴的模板

```text
Create a coherent 3x2 storyboard contact sheet with six clearly separated panels,
for a [15]-second scene. Read order: left to right, top to bottom.

CONTINUITY BIBLE — repeat in every panel:
Subject: [exact character: age band, face, hair, build, full outfit, key prop].
Location: [place with 2-4 fixed landmarks].
Time and light: [time of day, named light source, direction, color temperature].
Palette: [3-4 colors]. Screen direction: [left-to-right / right-to-left].

PANEL 1 — establishing wide: [environment and opening state].
PANEL 2 — medium: [first action beat].
PANEL 3 — close detail: [hand / product / emotion].
PANEL 4 — dynamic angle: [turn or escalation].
PANEL 5 — reaction: [payoff on the face or body].
PANEL 6 — clean hero frame: [final composition with negative space].

Thin white gutters between panels, no text, no captions, no numbering, no watermark.
Same wardrobe, same lighting logic, consistent proportions and screen direction.
Cinematic stills, shallow depth of field, [photorealistic / stylized 3D / anime] rendering.
Aspect ratio 16:9.
```

## 填写要点

- 六个格子要承担**不同的镜头功能**，不要六张都是“中景看着镜头”。
- 第 1 格建立空间，第 6 格留白给后期加字或做封面。
- 有产品时，把「产品锁定句」也抄进 Continuity Bible。

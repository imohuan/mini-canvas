# 人像质感调节

**LibTV 公开说明**：人像质感调节；同一入口还提供情绪调节。

**什么时候用**：人物已经对了，只想让皮肤、眼神、光线更自然。

## 可直接粘贴的模板

```text
Retouch this portrait while preserving the person's identity exactly.

IDENTITY LOCK: keep the same face geometry, eyes, nose, lips, jawline, ears, apparent age,
skin tone, freckles, moles, expression, gaze, pose, body proportions, clothing, framing,
and background. Do not reshape the face, do not slim or enlarge features, do not change age.

ALLOWED CHANGES:
- natural skin cleanup with visible pores and fine texture retained;
- even out patchy redness and minor blemishes without plastic smoothing;
- lift face exposure slightly and add soft catchlights to both eyes;
- balance white balance and recover detail in shadows/highlights;
- gentle sharpening on eyes, lashes, lips, and hair edges.

FORBIDDEN: beauty-filter skin, wax texture, over-whitened teeth, enlarged eyes, sharpened
chin, changed makeup style, changed hairline, changed expression, new jewelry, text, watermark.

Result must look like a professional natural retouch, not AI beautification. Keep original
aspect ratio and crop. Photorealistic, 85mm portrait compression, soft key light from
[camera-left / camera-right], subtle fill, natural skin texture.
```

## 情绪微调

需要换情绪时，只改面部肌肉和嘴部状态，不动骨相：

```text
Keep the same person and the same head angle. Change only the expression from [current]
to [target: gentle smile / quiet concern / surprised open mouth]. Move the brows, eyelids,
cheeks, and mouth corners minimally and anatomically. Do not change face shape, age, makeup,
hair, clothing, lighting, or camera.
```

## 填写要点

- “自然”不是形容词，要写“保留毛孔、细纹、肤色不均”。
- 先写保留清单，再写允许修改清单；顺序反过来最容易换脸。
- 一次只做一类调整：先皮肤，再光线；同一次全改会失去可控性。

# 角色设定图

**LibTV 公开说明**：角色主视觉与设定拆解，基于当前图像生成完整的角色设定图。

**区别于三视图**：三视图只解决“前后左右”，设定图要把气质、服装、道具、色卡一起讲清。

## 可直接粘贴的模板

```text
Create a professional character design sheet on a clean white background, for
[character name], [short role / archetype]. One coherent layout, no panels crossing.

IDENTITY LOCK: [age band, build, face shape, skin tone, eye color, hairstyle, distinguishing
marks]. The same character in every section — no variation in face, hair, or body proportions.

LAYOUT:
- Left third: full-body hero pose, front-facing, entire silhouette visible, feet on baseline.
- Upper right: detailed portrait bust at three-quarter angle, neutral expression.
- Middle right: three-quarter full-body pose showing the side silhouette and garment layers.
- Lower right: [3] signature props laid out at consistent scale, each separated.
- Bottom strip: [4-6] color swatches with hex codes; tiny name label in clean sans-serif.

WARDROBE: [exact outfit, fabrics, colors, closures, footwear, accessories]. Show seam lines
and material texture consistently in every section.

Lighting: soft even studio light, no dramatic shadow, no background scenery. No extra characters,
no decorative frame, no watermark. [photorealistic / cel-shaded anime / painterly] rendering.
Aspect ratio 3:4.
```

## 填写要点

- 角色设定图允许“主视觉 + 拆解”，但所有区块必须是**同一个角色**。
- 道具只放真正会反复出现的；道具太多会抢脸部细节。
- 色卡写 HEX，后续做场景和视频时可直接复用同一条色板。

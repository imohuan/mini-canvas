# 720全景

**LibTV 公开说明**：生成全景场景图，直接将场景图像转为 720 全景图；支持文生图 / 参考图。

**注意**：图像模型输出的是**一张 2:1 的等距圆柱全景图**；“720”指浏览器里可转动的视角体验，不是让图片本身变成球。

## 可直接粘贴的模板

```text
Create a 360-degree equirectangular panorama of [scene description].
Seamless 2:1 aspect ratio, equirectangular projection, full 360 degrees horizontally
and 180 degrees vertically, designed to be wrapped around an inverted sphere.

SCENE: [location, time of day, weather, season].
FIXED LANDMARKS: [north/front view], [east/right view], [south/back view], [west/left view].
CAMERA HEIGHT: [eye level / 1.6 m / low floor level], at the center of the space.
LIGHT: [named source], from [direction], [soft/hard], [color temperature].

Make the left edge continue exactly into the right edge with no visible seam, no duplicated
landmark, no warped horizon. Keep the ground and ceiling continuous. No fisheye circle,
no black vignette, no frame, no text, no watermark.
Photorealistic environment photography, even sharpness from foreground to background.
```

## 图生全景（已有场景图）

```text
Expand this reference image into a seamless 360-degree equirectangular panorama.
Keep the supplied image's architecture, materials, palette, and lighting unchanged.
Reconstruct the unseen 240 degrees around the camera consistently, with plausible exits,
windows, furniture, and ceiling. Match perspective and light direction. 2:1 aspect ratio,
seamless left-right wrap, no seam, no duplicated objects, no text, no watermark.
```

## 填写要点

- 一定写 `2:1`；其它比例做出来的全景会被拉伸。
- 先定四个方向的地标，模型才不会在接缝处复制同一棵树。
- 人像 / 角色不要放进全景：等距投影会把脸拉变形；需要角色时建议后期合成。
